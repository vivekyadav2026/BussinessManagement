Restaurant module

No app to install. Just a table, a QR, an order.

  • Menu

    Live digital menu

    Toggle any dish in or out of stock instantly — the customer's screen updates the same second.

  • Order

    Scan, browse, order

    Customer scans the table QR, adds to cart, and sends the order straight to the kitchen screen.

  • Kitchen

    Received → Preparing → Ready → Served

    Staff track every order's status in real time, then it converts into an invoice automatically.

  • Pay

    Pay online or at the counter

    Razorpay checkout at the table, or settle the bill after the meal — both close the same invoice.
